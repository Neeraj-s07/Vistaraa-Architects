Fifty three-storey homes on a five-acre gated estate. Private streets thread between the rows, each house turning inward to its own garden and lily pond, while the clubhouse and landscaped commons hold the centre of the plan.

Clubhouse, streets, landscape and all fifty homes were designed as one commission by the studio.
