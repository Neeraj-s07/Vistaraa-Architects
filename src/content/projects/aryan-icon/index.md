A glazed office tower on a corner plot in the MIDC district. Slim vertical fins run the full height of the curtain wall, interrupted at three levels by recessed planted terraces, while a double-height podium holds the arrival court at street level.

Tower, lobby and tenant floors were drawn by the studio as one continuous commission.
